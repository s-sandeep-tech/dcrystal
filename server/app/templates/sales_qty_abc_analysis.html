About this report

Sold Qty sums the source pieces for invoice rows across the selected period. Parent quantities sum their children.

The Contribution % next to Sold Qty uses the parent quantity. The Contribution % after Category uses the filtered report total. Siblings rank by quantity descending, then label and ID. Cumulative contribution restarts within each parent. A covers the first 80%, B the next 15%, and C the remainder. The row crossing a boundary stays in the category it completes. Zero-quantity rows are Unclassified.

Sold Weight includes invoice weights in kg. Net Turnover includes configured sales returns; Profit sums source TSK values. Profit/Pc divides invoice TSK by Sold Qty and displays in rupees. Profit % of Turnover divides total TSK by Net Turnover and is blank when Net Turnover is zero or negative. Turnover and Profit display in Cr with full-value tooltips. Transaction filters apply to every metric; selecting only returns produces no sold quantity.

Search reveals matching paths without recalculating ABC. The footer always shows the complete filtered total, counting each leaf once.
