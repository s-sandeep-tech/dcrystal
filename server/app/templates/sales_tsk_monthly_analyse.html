About this report

Share shows each group's contribution to monthly net turnover. Growth compares net turnover with the previous complete selected month. Sales Growth % is hidden for the first selected month. Missing or zero bases and partial-month comparisons show —. Months without transactions show zero totals.

Net turnover includes sales returns. Weight (kg), pieces, TSK, and MC Profit are source totals. Amounts display in Cr. Hover for full values. Share bars show contribution.
